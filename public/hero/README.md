# Hero photographs

Drop images in here and reference them from `defaultHeroSlides` in
`src/components/site/hero-carousel.tsx`:

```ts
{ kicker: "HIV prevention", caption: "We come to you", src: "/hero/01-testing.jpg", alt: "…" }
```

## What to shoot

There are twelve slots. The `alt` text on each slide in `hero-carousel.tsx` is
the brief — shoot to those.

Landscape, minimum 1920px wide. The left-hand ~45% of each frame sits under the
headline scrim, so keep your subject right of centre.

## Consent is not optional

UCC works in HIV, TB and GBV. Publishing a recognisable face alongside that
content can cost someone their job, their family or their safety — whether or
not they are actually a client.

- Written informed consent before any identifiable person is published.
- Consent to one use is not consent to all uses. A photo agreed for a report is
  not agreed for the website.
- Prefer hands, backs, wide shots and environment over faces.
- Never photograph a child without guardian consent, and prefer not to at all.

Stock photography is a poor substitute here: using an image of someone who has
no connection to HIV or GBV to illustrate those subjects is the same misuse of a
person's likeness the policy exists to prevent. Commission your own.
